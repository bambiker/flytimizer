Why altitude matters
  • The right height makes deliveries faster, keeps food warmer, and saves battery or stretches your range.
  • Fly often — a delivery route, say — and small gains per trip add up fast.
  • Wind isn't uniform with height, so a route grounded by a high-wind warning near the ground can still be flyable higher up.
  • We won't recommend flying below 30m — besides clearance margins, it also cuts noise disturbance noticeably. We'll keep you at least 20m above the tallest building OpenStreetMap knows about near your route, shown in faint orange on the map — climbing over buildings is simpler and safer than weaving between them at low level.
  • Schools, kindergartens, hospitals and playgrounds are risky to fly over — we mark the ones OpenStreetMap knows about in red on the map and route around them horizontally, since altitude alone doesn't make overflying them safe.
  • Altitude choice matters most on long routes, with higher-drag drones, and when the wind runs roughly along your heading.
  • Drag isn't the enemy — flown with the wind, it works for you.
